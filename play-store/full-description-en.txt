LK Studio helps tailoring and boutique shops grow with less effort.

For shop owners:
• Manage your design catalog with photos
• Receive and track customer orders with measurements
• Create bills, record advances and payments
• Share bills on WhatsApp
• Set shop profile, timings, location, and UPI details
• Monthly subscription with optional autopay

For customers:
• Find and save favourite shops
• Place orders with photos and measurement charts
• Track order status from measuring to ready for pickup
• View and pay bills
• Ask for price quotes on designs

Built for Indian tailoring businesses with support for English, Hindi, and Telugu.
