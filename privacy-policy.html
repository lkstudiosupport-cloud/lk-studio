LK Studio Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for LK Studio

Effective Date: 24 June 2026

LK Studio (“we,” “our,” or “us”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how LK Studio collects, uses, stores, and protects user information when you use the LK Studio mobile application.

1. About LK Studio

LK Studio is a studio and shop management application designed to help manage tailoring, blouse design, maggam work, customer orders, billing information, and shop details in one place.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the app is used, LK Studio may collect and store the following information:

a) Account and Contact Information

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Login details, if account-based access is enabled

b) Shop and Customer Management Information

  • Customer names and contact details entered by the shop
  • Order details and work status
  • Billing and payment-related records entered within the app
  • Shop-related details and records maintained by the user

c) Design and Media Information

  • Design images, uploaded files, or reference materials added to the app
  • Information related to browsing, viewing, or sharing design collections

d) Technical Information

  • Basic device or app usage information required to operate and improve the application
  • Error logs or technical diagnostics, if applicable

3. How We Use the Information

  • Provide and maintain LK Studio app functionality
  • Manage customer orders, billing records, and shop operations
  • Display, organize, and share design information within the app
  • Improve app performance, reliability, and user experience
  • Provide user support and resolve technical issues
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse of the application

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored within LK Studio. Data may be stored securely using trusted cloud or backend services required for app functionality. While we work to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Data Sharing

LK Studio does not sell personal information to third parties.

We may share information only when necessary for:

  • Providing app functionality through trusted service providers or hosting platforms
  • Legal compliance, if required by law
  • Protecting the rights, safety, and security of the app, users, or business operations

6. User-Provided Business Data

LK Studio may allow users to enter and manage customer, order, design, and billing information for their own business operations. Users are responsible for ensuring that the data they enter into the app is collected and used in accordance with applicable laws and with appropriate customer consent where required.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide app functionality, maintain business records within the app, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce app-related requirements.

8. Children’s Privacy

LK Studio is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on how LK Studio is configured and used, users may request updates or deletion of their account-related information by contacting us using the details below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted through the app or on the page where this Privacy Policy is published. Continued use of LK Studio after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
